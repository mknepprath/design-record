# Owner's constraints for the showtime window app

Statements from the owner, quoted as written.

| # | Quote | Bears on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "most movie theater apps assume you're choosing based on the movie and not based on the time you have available" | The problem |
| 2 | "how would i go about building an app that let me pick the time window I have available and show me the exact shortlist of movies available to watch within that time, accounting for trailers as well." | The goal |
| 3 | "a web app is fine to start" | Platform |
| 4 | "I would like a solid plan and I don't have money to invest beyond hosting the site itself at the moment" | Budget: $0 beyond hosting |
| 5 | "we can assess which theatesr are the lowest hanging fruit and maybe just support one to start if there's an easy one" | Start with one source |
| 6 | "Cinemark is near me, no alamos" | First source |

Quote 1 is the owner's claim. In the same conversation it was checked only against recall of AMC, Regal, Cinemark, Fandango, Atom and Google showtimes, not against the apps themselves.
